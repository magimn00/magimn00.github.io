03

The Hartwell House: A Museum in VR

A museum in VR, built in Unity 6. Visitors walk and teleport through the Hartwell House, the home of the fictional author and illustrator Eleanor Hartwell, and learn her story from the objects in it. Exhibits show a label when you point at them, and you can switch on the radio, lights, and TV and turn the pages of a book.

Unity 6VRXR Interaction ToolkitMeta Quest

Environment: Stylized House Interior by StylArts, made by artist Phil Stoltz, with some concept references by Eduardo Fornieles.
