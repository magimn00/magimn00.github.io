Affective Gestures for Pedagogical Agents

Animated pedagogical agents performing affective arm and hand gestures extracted from the GEMEP dataset. A perception study with 131 participants examined how viewers recognize emotion, arousal, and valence from gesture alone.

Body Type & Voice Pitch in Virtual Characters

A within-group study (72 participants) testing how combinations of body type and voice pitch affect perceived audio-visual correspondence and believability of virtual characters. Published at the ACM Symposium on Applied Perception 2023.
