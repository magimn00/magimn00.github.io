    Mukanova, M., Yang, F.-C., Adamo, N., & Mousas, C.

  • Journal · 2024

    Recognizing and Relating to the Race/Ethnicity and Gender of Animated Pedagogical Agents

    Zhao, F., Mayer, R. E., Adamo-Villani, N., Mousas, C., Choi, M., Lam, L., Mukanova, M., & Hauser, K.

    Journal of Educational Computing Research, 62(3), 455–481

    Examines how learners recognize and relate to the race/ethnicity and gender of animated pedagogical agents, contributing to research on agent appearance, identity perception, and learner–agent interaction.

  • Conference · 2024

    Animated Pedagogical Agents Performing Affective Gestures Extracted from the GEMEP Dataset: Can People Recognize Their Emotions?

    Mukanova, M., Adamo-Villani, N., Mousas, C., Zhao, F., Choi, M., Lam, L., & Hauser, K.

    ArtsIT 2023 · Lecture Notes of the Institute for Computer Sciences, Social Informatics and Telecommunications Engineering, vol. 565, Springer

    Investigates whether people can recognize emotions expressed by animated pedagogical agents through affective arm and hand gestures extracted from the GEMEP dataset.

  • Conference · 2023

    Effects of Body Type and Voice Pitch on Perceived Audio-Visual Correspondence and Believability of Virtual Characters

    Lam, L., Choi, M., Mukanova, M., Hauser, K., Zhao, F., Mayer, R., Mousas, C., & Adamo-Villani, N.

    ACM Symposium on Applied Perception 2023, Article 3

    A within-group study with 72 participants showing that voice pitch and its interaction with body type affect perceived audio-visual correspondence and believability — appropriate voice–body matching makes virtual characters more believable.

    DOI ↗
  • Thesis · 2023

    Affective Arm and Hand Gestures in Animated Pedagogical Agents

    Mukanova, M.

    M.S. Thesis · ProQuest Dissertations and Theses

    An online perception study with 131 participants examined recognition of six basic emotions from gesture-driven agent animation. Recognition of specific emotions was low, but participants identified broader dimensions such as arousal and valence more accurately.
