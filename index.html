Research

What I Work On

I am particularly interested in how subtle motion cues — gesture size, timing, posture, and facial behavior — influence human perception of virtual characters. My projects bridge animation practice with scientific evaluation.

  • Personality expression in virtual humans
  • Familiarity & trust in embodied agents
  • Behavioral animation systems
  • Motion perception & social cues
  • Conversational MetaHuman agents
  • Gesture generation & procedural animation
  • Motion capture workflows
